# Video Presentation

- **Duration:** 3–5 minutes. Aim for 4:30. You will lose marks if you go over 5:00 minutes.
- **Platform:** YouTube, visibility set to Unlisted, titled with your team name only.

## What to Cover (Suggestion Only)

| Topic | What to cover |
| --- | --- |
| Your solution and what's different | What it is, then what's novel about it. Be direct about the twist. You can even merge this with the prototype demo and explain the novel features as you are showing the screens that represent them. |
| Prototype demo | You can screen-record the real flow and narrate it end to end or have images within the slides. Point out design decisions as you reach them.<br>**NOTE:** Prototype demo does not mean you need working logic, your prototype is your UI design, and the demo is you walking through the important screens. |
| Tech stack and build plan | Frontend, backend, database, services, hosting. What you'll build in the building phase (3 weeks). |
| Impact and close | What concretely changes for your user. End on why this is worth building. |

> **Not needed here:** Your ideation process, mentor consultations, and idea evolution are judged from your README. No need to spend video time on them, the video should be focused on making the reviewer what your project is, not how you got there.

> **Reminder:** You have the link the slides in your README.md too.
