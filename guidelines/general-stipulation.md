# General Stipulations

Covering platform and tech flexibility.

- Mobile applications may be developed for iOS only, Android only, or both. Teams are not required to support both platforms.
- Mobile solutions may be built as native apps (Swift, Kotlin, Java, etc.) or using cross-platform frameworks (Flutter, React Native, etc.).
- Where a problem statement says "Mobile Application and/or Website," teams may choose to build either one, or both. A web-only or mobile-only submission is acceptable.
- Web-based solutions may be built as traditional websites or as browser-based apps. Responsive design for mobile browsers is encouraged.
- Teams are free to use any tech stack, framework, or language they're comfortable with.
- Solutions must be deployable. Submissions should not rely solely on running in a local development environment as teams should be able to demonstrate the app running on an actual device, emulator, or hosted environment.
- Use of third-party APIs, SDKs, or paid services is allowed, provided free-tier or trial access is available for demo purposes. Teams are responsible for their own API keys and usage limits.
- Use of AI tools and APIs for development or as part of the solution itself is permitted where relevant but teams must be aware of the inner-workings of the projects they submit.
- Teams may use pre-existing boilerplate, templates, or open-source libraries, but the core logic and features addressing the problem statement must be built during the hackathon.
- Accessibility considerations (e.g. screen reader support, text-to-speech, colour contrast) are encouraged where mentioned in a problem statement, but partial implementation is acceptable given time constraints.
- All submissions must be original work created during the hackathon period. Prior personal projects may not be resubmitted.
