# Contributing

Quick rules for the team while we build.

## Branches

`main` is protected, no direct pushes or force-pushes. Branch off per piece of work:

- `feature/<short-desc>` - new screens or functionality
- `fix/<short-desc>` - bug fixes

## Pull Requests

1. Push your branch, open a PR into `main`.
2. Get at least one teammate to look it over before merging.
3. Squash or regular merge, your call - just don't force-push over `main`.
